# HORIZON — War Room to a hypothetical v20

Written 2026-07-12 (Fable finale session), at Greg's request, before the v5
sprint opens. This is an EXPLORATORY arc, not a contract: v-numbers are
eras, not promises. Grounded in the real trajectory so far — v0 viewer →
v1 game+dispatch → v3 Living Studio face → v4 console → v5 finish/connect
(draft) — and in recorded strategic facts: War Room is Greg's intended
PRIMARY orchestration interface; commercial sell-shovels ambition is real
(fund Tbilisi); Mac Mini = compute node; kill-criterion reframed to
HARVEST hosted Agent View plumbing if Anthropic ships it.

## The one-sentence thesis

The arc bends from **watching agents** (done) → **steering agents** (v5–7)
→ **governing a fleet under policy** (v8–11) → **running Greg's whole
estate from anywhere** (v12–15) → **a world that plans itself, with Greg
as the gate** (v16–20).

## Act I — FINISH THE CONSOLE (v5–v7)

_"Every project, every agent, one room. Greg stops opening terminals."_

- **v5 (drafted, gates pending):** districts real sources, sprite
  diversity + asset skill, born-managed wrapper DESIGN, old-face
  retirement, gate integrity. (`KICKOFF-v5-DRAFT.md`)
- **v6 — The city:** every `~/code` project is a building fed by its own
  STATE.md/gh signals (wiring auto-detect grows into the city map);
  morning-push consolidation lands (CROSS-SYSTEM idea #1) — ONE Bark push
  opens the board, which absorbs the morning page. The board becomes the
  first thing Greg sees every day, by design instead of by habit.
- **v7 — Steering:** "born managed" becomes the default way Greg's own
  sessions start (launcher wrapper from the v5 design gate) → ANY session

- **Act exit question:** did terminal-in-Obsidian usage actually drop to
  ~zero? If not, the console thesis is wrong somewhere — find where

## Act II — FROM CONSOLE TO GOVERNOR (v8–v11)

_"Autonomy with receipts. The ladder grows rungs, never skips them."_

- **v8 — Policy plane:** the T3 auto-executor whitelist matures into
  per-project POLICIES (auto-approve read-lane actions, gate writes,
  budget envelopes per project/day); standing orders replace personal
  cron scripts; every policy decision emits a verbatim receipt + undo.
- **v9 — Multi-model as a primitive:** judge panels, reviewer-on-return,
  and cross-model tournaments become board verbs, not orchestrator
  habits — a dispatch can CARRY its reviewer; disagreements render as
  crises with both transcripts attached. (The P5/P6 codex saves proved
  this pattern earns its cost.)
- **v10 — Self-ops:** today's INFRA-AUDIT agent becomes a scheduled
  resident — the estate probes itself (mounts, runners, clones, cron,
  watchdog) and files its own honest-red crises; NEXUS health is a
  building on the board.
- **v11 — The knowledge loop closes:** session transcripts distill into
  vault knowledge automatically (graphify pipeline as a post-session
  routine); the knowledge graph becomes the board's long-term memory;
  "what did we decide about X, and in which session" is a SEARCH-prop
  answer with receipts.
- **Act exit question:** after the first real autonomous mistake (there
  will be one), did the receipts + undo actually make it cheap? Autonomy
  budget expands ONLY as fast as recovery stays boring.

## Act IV — THE WORLD IS THE INTERFACE (v16–v20)

_"The office stops depicting the work and starts BEING the control plane."_

- **v16 — Living identity:** generative asset pipeline matures — every
  coworker has a stable, earned visual identity (provider/model/history);
  the office architecture itself accretes from real usage (rooms exist
  because work happened there — real-progress doctrine, spatialized).
- **v17 — Ambient grammar:** the soundscape becomes information (a chirp
  grammar you can learn eyes-free); wall-display glance mode for the
  Tbilisi flat; Bark evolves into watch-grade complications. The board
  reaches Greg without being asked.
- **v18 — Rehearsal:** dry-run dispatches ("what WOULD the fleet do"),
  incident replay theater reconstructed from receipts, and pre-mortem
  sims for risky operations — the honest-data doctrine extended to
  futures: simulations always labeled ⊘ SIMULATED.
- **v19 — The economy fork:** the board's cash either becomes REAL
  (contract payouts = invoices, budget = actual API spend, one ledger) or
  is permanently declared a game. This is a Greg values-gate, not a
  technical one — v19 exists to force the choice honestly.
- **v20 — The console governs itself:** a standing "orchestrator" managed
  session lives in the office; the board dispatches its own roadmap
  agents, drafts v21, and Greg's job reduces to the gates — approve,
  redirect, kill — from any device, anywhere. v20 plans v21 the way this
  document was itself produced: by the system, for its operator.

## Invariants (violate any of these and it isn't War Room anymore)

1. **Honest data.** No fabricated states, no plausible zeros, no
   completion theater — ⊘ NO DATA over pretty lies, at every scale.
2. **Capability is local.** The wire carries intent; machines (and later,
   tenants) hold their own allowlists. Deny by default, forever.
3. **Colorblind-safe.** Shape + word first; color never load-bearing.
4. **Greg gates the irreversible.** Autonomy grows in receipts-backed
   rungs; deploys, deletions, money, and publishing stay human-gated
   until a policy EXPLICITLY says otherwise per-action-class.
5. **Real progress only.** Buildings, floors, cash, trophies — all derive
   from observed events. The v19 fork decides what "real" means for
   money; it never un-decides it for everything else.
6. **The vault stays upstream.** Read-only consumption unless a future
   Greg-gate creates a specific, audited write path.
