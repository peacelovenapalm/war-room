# HORIZON question register — 2026-07-12

Greg asked to be prompted with 30–50 shaping questions before the horizon
hardens. Answer by number, any subset, any order — one-phrase answers are
fine; skipped numbers mean "orchestrator's judgment." Answers get folded
into HORIZON-v20.md and the v6+ kickoffs.

## A · Mornings & the ambient spine

- **Q1.** Walk me through your ideal first 10 minutes awake — where does the board appear, and what must it have already decided FOR you?
- **Q2.** How many pushes per day are acceptable before Bark becomes noise? One? Three? Severity-gated?
- **Q3.** Should the morning surface be the BOARD itself (iso world) or a flat text-first brief with the board one tap away?
- **Q4.** Is there a physical wall display in the Tbilisi plan (spare monitor/tablet), or is "glanceable" always phone-sized?
- **Q5.** Sound: do you actually WANT an audible grammar (chirps you learn), or is sound forever optional garnish you keep muted?
- **Q6.** What's the ONE number/state you'd want on a watch face or lock screen, if only one fits?
- **Q7.** When nothing needs you, should the system say so ("all calm, nothing today") or stay silent? Which builds more trust for you?

## B · Memory that compounds

- **Q8.** When you ask "what did we decide about X" — do you want the ANSWER, or the answer + receipts (session, date, diff) every time?

- **Q10.** Who owns the write path for auto-distilled knowledge — the routine plane (PR you merge, like vault-fixer) or direct-to-vault with an audit trail?
- **Q11.** How should WRONG remembered knowledge die — manual delete only, staleness decay (◷ after N days unconfirmed), or contradiction-detection flags?

- **Q13.** Should memory span beyond code — Waypoint, health, finances from the vault — or stay strictly project/infra scoped?
