## Methodology note

Evidence gathered by direct file inspection (`Read`/`grep`) of
`webview-ui/src/**` (v1, 13-control `BottomToolbar.tsx` + `App.tsx`
mount tree) against `webview-v3/src/**` (v3, confirmed via
`git show aedf6f9 --stat` — the WS-A merge commit's own message says
"13 panel ports," matching the count here). Decision-register citations
are line-anchored to `.planning/v3/KICKOFF-v3.1.md` §1 and
`.planning/v2/GAME-DESIGN-V3.md`. One cross-check: `.planning/v2/STATE-v2.0.md`
row G-1 records Greg's live gate instruction verbatim — "do NOT drop v1
tabs/surface (HELP, SHIFT, BRIEFING, drawer, decor, progression)" — which
is why decor placement (#2a) and the progression replacement (#16) are
scored GAP rather than silently accepted as out of scope: an explicit
instruction to keep them exists, and no later decision-register line
retracts it for _this_ sprint.
